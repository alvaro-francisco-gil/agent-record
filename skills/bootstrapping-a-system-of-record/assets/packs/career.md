# Pack: career

A person's working life: who they are professionally, what they have done, what they
are aiming at, and the documents that prove it. Empty structure only. Every row is a
suggestion the interview keeps, renames or drops.

## Typical renderings

CVs and résumés, cover letters, bios for programmes and profiles, application answers,
portfolio pages. Received: contracts, diplomas and certificates, reference letters.

## Routing rows to offer

| Fact | Suggested destination |
|---|---|
| Current situation, role, constraints, what they want next | `identity/PROFILE.md` (the entry point) |
| Positioning, themes, the story they tell | `identity/narrative.md` |
| Each past role or engagement: dates, scope, outcomes | `experience/<organisation>.md` |
| Education, certifications, languages | `credentials/` |
| Applications in flight and their outcomes | `applications/<date>-<organisation>.md` |
| Generated CVs and letters | `renderings/` |
| Signed contracts, diplomas as received | `documents/` |

## Questions this domain needs

- What are you looking for next, and what would make you say no?
- Which past roles do you get asked about most, and what can you prove about each?
- Where do your diplomas, contracts and references live today?
- Is there anything about a past employer you must not write down, such as a
  confidentiality clause?

## Sensitive categories to raise in question 3

Compensation history, identity documents, reasons for leaving, health, other people's
contact details, confidential client or employer material.

## Skills that often help

`plans-lifecycle`, when applications or a career change are tracked as ongoing work.
The obligations register, when certificates or visas expire.
